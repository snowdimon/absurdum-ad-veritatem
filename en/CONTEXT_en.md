## Interdisciplinary Context: Absurdum ad Veritatem (The Kafka Test)

### Cybernetics & Systems Theory
Systems are usually tested for resilience to external disturbances (Ashby's Law). The Kafka Test proposes a check for an internal flaw: what if a system's rule is executed with absolute precision, pushed to the grotesque? This is a test of the contradiction between the design intent and its ultimate embodiment.

### Philosophy
Absurdism (Camus, Kafka) and reductio ad absurdum. The Kafka Test does not prove a rule false (like classical reductio). It reveals a hidden defect and demands a safeguard. This is not the destruction of an idea, but its completion into an honest form.

### Psychology
Cognitive biases and rigidity. The Kafka Test is a tool for identifying unconscious rules that govern behavior («obey your elders», «be strong») which, when taken to the absolute, destroy the personality.
